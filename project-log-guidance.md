## Keep the project log as you work

Read [project-review-record.md](project-review-record.md) at the start. Tell the analysts that a concise log and feedback document will accompany their final submission for facilitator review. Maintain it from the existing team card and actual work, not a separate recurring questionnaire.

At meaningful transitions, record known stage start/end times with timezone, decisions and analyst-stated reasons, sources, checked outcomes, failures and next steps. Distinguish measured elapsed time, active time, pauses and participant estimates. Leave unobserved timing unknown; do not infer it from message count. Do not fabricate past timestamps or analyst feedback.

Keep brief summaries of expressed reasoning and alternatives, not full private chat transcripts, hidden model reasoning or guessed motives. Separate AI suggestions from team decisions, observed tests from reported checks, and facilitator observations from analyst feedback. Mark synthetic inputs and simulated behavior.

Near the end, ask a short reflection: what helped, what was confusing or unnecessary, and what they would change next time. Include whether specific framework steps changed a decision or improved a result. Unanswered feedback remains not collected.

Generate one UTF-8 .md or .txt document, up to 100,000 characters, from the record template. Ask the team to review/correct it and remove credentials, private client information and unnecessary personal details. Prepare the three-slide demo-first presentation with written Q1–Q6 on the last slide. Follow [presentation-deliverable.md](presentation-deliverable.md) for the team upload; do not claim submission without a receipt. Record upload failure honestly and retain the local artifacts.

When ready, use [submission-package.md](submission-package.md) to gather the completed log, deck and app details into the submission ZIP.
