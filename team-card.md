# Build Lab team card

Use this one-page working aid during the two-hour lab. Short answers and evidence links are enough. The longer templates in the guide are reference for real projects, not extra lab work.

<!-- CARD -->
**Team / assigned project:**  
**App URL / version demonstrated:**  
**Current responsibility:** build ___ / checks ___ / next decision ___

| Question | Short answer and evidence |
|---|---|
| **Q1: Problem and app** | User and problem; supported workflow; important limit. |
| **Q2: AI and people** | One task AI handled; one decision/check your team owned. If relevant, distinguish AI used to build the app from AI or automation used when it runs. |
| **Q3: Assumption** | What you investigated, what you found, and what changed or was confirmed. Include the chosen mockup and reason if relevant. |
| **Q4: Verification** | Expected → observed normal and failure behavior; evidence link; what remains unverified. |
| **Q5: Failure and response** | Actual incident or deliberate test; response; checkpoint and recheck. Say what was not tested. |
| **Q6: Improve the process** | One change to how you would use AI and how you would measure its effect. |

**Shareback:** three slides: problem/app; app demo; written Q1–Q6 answers for review (not required in the live talk). Present for 5–7 minutes, with an app demonstration; answer all six questions in writing on the final slide, which does not need to be presented. Submit the app URL, editable PowerPoint and reviewed project log/feedback through the hub. Reuse this card to prepare the log; do not submit duplicate notes.

**Final handoff:** Ask Codex to prepare your submission ZIP. Review the files in `submission/`, load the ZIP in the hub, and keep the confirmed receipt.
